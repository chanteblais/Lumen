# art/

Source art, never served. Eight folders, and one that is not committed:

- `lumi/` — Lumi's source drawings, the sheets her sprites are cut from. Kept in the repo so the cut can be redone.
- `prompts/` — one file per generated sheet (`<sheet>.md`): the brief, the settings, the prompt verbatim, what changed version to version, and every run with its verdict. `scripts/gen-lumi-sheet.py` reads it, calls OpenAI's image API and appends the runs; the method is `docs/animation-pipeline.md` → Prompt lab.
- `candidates/` — gitignored: what the generator returned, with each candidate's measurements and aligned strip. The approved sheet moves to `lumi/`.
- `scenery/` — the painted rooms, one folder per place: its `background.png` (the painting served behind the page) and anything else that belongs to that place. Every background is served as `public/<place>-room.webp`, 1536×1024 at quality 84; regenerate after a repaint with `python3 -c "from PIL import Image; Image.open('art/scenery/<place>/background.png').convert('RGB').save('public/<place>-room.webp','WEBP',quality=84,method=6)"`.
  - `scenery/home/` — the lamplit room behind Home (`public/home-room.webp`; `docs/design-system.md` → Home: the room), repainted 2026-09-12 to give Lumi more floor to wander.
  - `scenery/today/` — the greenhouse behind Today (`public/today-room.webp`; → Today: the garden).
  - `scenery/library/` — the reading room behind the Library, its signs and banners blank: `background-iso.png`, the isometric room served as `public/library-room.webp` since 2026-09-13 (→ Library: the reading room), with a small reading circle; `background.png`, the frontal room it replaced (spatial map v1–v2 were drawn on it); `book-template.png`, the open book a Thread is set in, empty; and `library-spatial-information-architecture.md`, Chanté's spatial IA for the Library (Library → Collection → Thread Group → Thread). `shelf-zoom.png`, the reading scale a Collection close-up aims for (Thread Group plaques and titles on spines, both to be drawn in code); `library-views.json`, the proposed camera-view manifest (collection slots, Thread Group rows, close-up frames, Lumi's anchors; not yet approved), drawn onto the painting in `spatial-map/` by `scripts/draw-library-map.py`.
- `assets/` — Chanté's asset sheets in progress, not cut or served yet: `icon-sheet.png` (1536×1024), sixty painted icons in brass, parchment and forest green on a soft brown ground, six rows of ten — the places (house, sun, open book, sprig, compass, gear), speech, search, arrows, sparkles, refresh, lists and sliders, moon and sun; scrolls, books, a bookmark, heart, hourglass, bell, calendar; tea, leaves, a lotus, a target, a mind, an eye, crystals, mountains, a path; folder, briefcase, home, people, clock, document, link, copy, bin; and a plant from seed to bloom and wilt, with a watering can, shears, a seed sack and a terrarium.
- `ui/` — Chanté's painted interface pieces, both painted on a checkerboard drawn into the pixels (not transparent): `nav-rail.png`, the nav's green rail, and `nav-parchment.png`, the sheet the nav's names are on. `scripts/cut-nav-art.py` keys each off its checkerboard and cuts it into a head, a stretching body and a foot in `public/` (`nav-rail-*.webp`, `nav-parchment-*.webp`); rerun it after a repaint and keep the measurements in its docstring and `globals.css` (`.nav-rail`, `.nav-toggle`, `.nav-mark`, `.nav-panel::before`) in step.
- `mockups/` — the page mockups the scenes are built toward; reference only, never served. Home (`home.png`); Today (`today-mockup.png`); the Library room with its boards named (`library-mockup.png`) and as first painted, with words on its signs (`library-background.png`); a Collection close-up (`shelf-zoom.png`); a Thread as an open book (`book-mockup-1.png`, `book-mockup2.png`, `book_layout-mockup.png`); and a pose sheet of Lumi's idles and actions (`lumi-poses.png` — poses, not in-betweens, never cut); and the nav as a green rail with a parchment sheet beside it, over the Library room (`rail-nav.png`, built in code 2026-09-13).

## Adding a sheet (what the playful foot taught us, 2026-09-12)

**Ask for in-betweens of one drawing, not poses.** A sheet where every frame is a fresh drawing never reads as motion, whatever the cut does: the hood, ribbon and folds boil, and the moving part covers its travel in one step. The lantern sheet is such a sheet (a pose set with expressions), which is why its body is one cell plus a synthesised breath. The prompt that worked for the foot (then ChatGPT image generation by hand, now `scripts/gen-lumi-sheet.py` with the prompt in `art/prompts/`; the lantern sheet's rest cell, cropped off the sheet, as the pose/style reference):

- ONE base drawing; change only what moves in each phase. Name the phases and their frame ranges, and say which parts hold "pixel for pixel" during each.
- Twelve or more frames per motion, small even steps, "slow in, slow out"; frame 1 = the rest pose; last frame = frame 1.
- A regular grid (2 or 3 rows of 8), same cell size, generous margins, the figure in the same place in every cell, feet on one baseline.
- A flat, untextured, contrasting ground (the grey-blue of the current sheets); no titles, labels, numbers or notes anywhere.
- Same style, line weight, palette, lighting and costume as the reference — the sun on the hood, the ribbon with its brass medallions, the boots, the lantern, and no scarf (the canon costume, `docs/art-direction.md` §2); highest resolution, landscape.

**Measure before judging** — `python3 scripts/measure-lumi-sheet.py art/lumi/<sheet>.png` prints per-frame geometry, the eye position (a glance), the per-step head change (IoU) and the scale per row, and writes an aligned strip to flip through and a motion GIF (the frames anchored on the hood's right edge and the feet, 120 ms a frame, `LUMI_MS` to change) — the sheet's motion before any cut, which is what Chanté approves. Crop the sheet to its rows of cells first if it carries a title or notes (the lantern sheet does: rows at y 151–393 and 444–687). What the generator gets wrong, and what the cut already fixes:

| Seen on every sheet so far | Fix, in `scripts/cut-lumi-idle.py` |
|---|---|
| A whole phase (row) drawn at a different scale | scale each row to `FIGURE_H` (hood top to feet) |
| A phase not drawn, a stray rest frame mid-row | play what exists, forward then in reverse; skip the stray — the order lives in `LumiSprite` → `LUMI_LOOP_CELLS` |
| The hood rim and its sun redrawn a little every frame (IoU ~0.98) | hold one frame's head across the phase (the foot cut's `hold_head`, in git history) |
| Frames a pixel off each other | settle on the head silhouette to a quarter pixel (the foot cut's `settle`, in git history) |
| A cold shadow and a grey rim from the contrasting ground | shadow found by its blue cast and redrawn warm; edges de-matted against the ground. For the hands-free Lumi the shadow is cut away instead (`Sheet(shadow=False)`) and the page draws it — a baked cream shadow looked pasted on over a painted floor (`docs/design-system.md` → Lumi companion) |
| The lantern's light pooling on the ground | found by its warm cast, lifted off as a translucent warm glow (`GLOW_RGB`, `GLOW_MAX`) |
| No blink row | lids drawn down over her own eyes (`eyes_shut`: half-shut keeps the bottom half, shut a thin lens; the eye's halo on the face goes with the lid) |
| No in-betweens at all | the rest cell alone, stretched ≤ 2px at the hood top with the feet held, nine frames (`breathe`) |
| A gesture widens the figure on one side (the waving hand), so centring on the body slides her | cells placed by the edge that holds — the hood's right edge (`hood_right`) |
| The matte's lowest row misses the feet in some cells (five wave cells dropped 2–4px), and the redraw moves the boots, lantern and eyes a pixel or two — she jumps | aligned on the parts that hold, both axes (`align`), then held: every cell keeps the first cell's pixels except where the moving part is (`hold`). Check with the held parts' positions on the *cut* cells, not the head overlap alone |
| Inside the moving part the redraw still bobs what should hold for a phase: the hem dipped ~2px before lifting and shifted 1–2px through the waves — she jitters up and down | holds per phase: rest cells are the rest cell; a held phase keeps one cell except the part that moves in it (`hold_hand`); the lower cloak moves one way through a rise or a fall (`monotone_lower`, keeping either frame's hand out of the blend). Measure on alpha, which the WebP keeps lossless, not on colour thresholds |
| Blink rows for every cell of a long gesture (the wave's would have tripled the sheet) | none: a short gesture keeps its eyes open (`LUMI_LOOP_EYES` in LumiSprite) |
| Lines ruled between the cells (five of eight grid candidates, 2026-09-13) — a row reads as one figure | painted over before segmentation, in the measure script and `lumi_cut.Sheet`: a thin line (plain ground 4px either side) across most of the sheet |
| Ground enclosed between the boots, which the flood from outside can't reach — a grey-blue patch between her feet | ground-coloured holes in her lowest quarter cut out (`lumi_cut.Sheet.matte`) |
| Loops from different sheets draw the cloak a little differently (overlap 0.95 against the rest drawing), so it swaps shape where a loop leaves the rest cell | every loop held to one rest drawing, and the two cells next to a rest cell eased from it into their own, anything dark kept out of the blend (`cut-lumi-free.py`, `ease`) |
| With no baked shadow, the boots' edge sits on bare floor, and boots redrawn a little differently by each sheet jitter as loops hand over (0.25–0.38px) | the boots below the hem held from the rest drawing in every cell, the breath included, except where the cell's own cloak hangs over them (`hold_feet`); regions run to the ground so a hand that leaves the hem leaves no stub. Measure the boots from the hem down, not only the last rows |
| A prop in the sheet that isn't in the rest drawing (the book stack) pops in at the hand-over | kept in the loop's rest cells and faded in over three cells before the loop; played backwards, out (`prop`, `PROP_FADE`) |
| A prop standing apart from her is segmented as extra figures | the closest spans in a row are joined until there are as many as the grid asks for (`lumi_cut.Sheet.frames`) |

**Then wire it:** for the hands-free Lumi, a spec in `LOOPS` (`scripts/cut-lumi-free.py`) and a run; a name in `LUMI_LOOPS`, an order in `LUMI_LOOP_CELLS` and its eye rows in `LUMI_LOOP_EYES` (LumiSprite; `LUMI_LOOP_FRAMES` is derived); a duration in `FRAME_MS` and the name in `VARIATIONS`, `REACTIONS` or `ACTIONS` (LumiCompanion); a cue button appears in the dev strip on its own. Frame fades last 80% of the faster loop's frame time — a fade longer than the frame reads as blur. Look first with `python3 scripts/preview-lumi-loop.py` (a strip and a GIF of the loop as the page plays it, and the held parts measured on alpha with a pass line; `--sheet public/lumi-free.webp --w 176 --row <sheet row> --eye-rows 1` for a hands-free loop), then on port 3005/3006 with the dev cue strip — never in the Claude-in-Chrome tab (Chrome freezes animation there).
