Lumi walks Home

Five drawings of her at the room’s angle, three of them mirrored, so she can face eight ways. She walks only along those eight, turns through the drawings between, and code moves the pieces: boots that step, legs drawn from under the cloak to each boot, a cloak that sways. Click the floor to send her somewhere, or let her walk the tour.

What to look at

  • She faces the way she walks, whichever of the eight ways it is — no walking sideways.
  • At a corner she stops and turns through the drawings in between, each a moment long. A quarter turn is one drawing; half round is three.
  • Her legs run from under the cloak to her boots every step, and the slit of her coat stays filled.
  • The table and cushions, as before: mapped by hand, and still the rough part.

Her height is in the painting’s own pixels; the table stands about 65. Heights and pace here are for judging, not settings in the app.
