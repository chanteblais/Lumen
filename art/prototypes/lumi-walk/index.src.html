Lumi walks Home

Drawings of her at the room’s angle, a drawing for every sixteenth of a turn, half of them mirrored. She walks eight ways, rounds corners on the move and turns through the drawings between; code moves the pieces: boots that step, legs drawn to each boot, a cloak that sways and trails a turn. Click the floor to send her somewhere, or let her walk the tour.

What to look at

  • A corner of a quarter turn or less, she rounds on the move, changing drawing every sixteenth of a turn. A sharper one, she stops, her hood leans toward the new way, and she steps round.
  • Turn: Swap changes drawing on a small squash; Blend fades the new drawing in over 70 ms. Which reads smoother, and does Blend ghost?
  • She faces the way she walks. Turn on the floor plan: a red arrow at her feet marks where her face points (measured on each drawing), a white one where she is walking.
  • Her legs run from under the cloak to her boots every step.

Her height is in the painting’s own pixels; the table stands about 65. Heights, pace and the turn style here are for judging, not settings in the app.
