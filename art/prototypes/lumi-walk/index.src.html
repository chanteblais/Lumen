Lumi walks Home

Two drawings of her, one turned toward you and one turned away, each mirrored for the other side. Code moves the pieces: her boots step, the cloak sways and the hood follows a beat late. Click the floor to send her somewhere, or let her walk the tour.

What to look at

  • Her boots: each one stays put on the floor while it carries her, then lifts and steps.
  • Behind the low table, the tabletop, the lantern and the plant cover her; in front of the cushions, she covers them.
  • At a corner she stops, dips and changes drawing. Does that read as turning round?
  • The hood and the hem of the cloak trail her sway by a beat. Too much, too little?

Her height is in the painting's own pixels; the table stands about 65. Heights and pace here are for judging, not settings in the app.
