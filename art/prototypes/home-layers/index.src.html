Home in layers

The same painting taken apart: an empty room (the furniture cut out and the floor painted in behind it), each piece of furniture as its own layer standing on a ground footprint, and the shadows it casts drawn on the floor under everything. A stand-in Lumi is sorted against the pieces by where their footprints meet the floor. Click the floor to put her somewhere, or drag her.

What to look at

  • Close behind the low table, where the hand-traced map failed: the tabletop and what's on it should cover her lower half and nothing else.
  • In front of anything, nothing of it should be drawn over her, and no shadow should darken her.
  • Plate only: seams where painted-in floor meets the painting — the rug's flowers and border, plank lines, the window's light patches.
  • Layers tinted shows what each layer holds; look for floor carried along with a piece (a halo or dark fringe) and for thin things — chair legs, spindles, leaves — gone patchy.
  • Original painting cuts between the painting and the rebuilt room: the two should be the same picture.

Footprints are where each piece meets the floor (cyan), extents the widest part dropped to the floor (white), the walkable floor dashed, and anchors — proposals — in red. Her cloak's width at her feet is the short red line.
