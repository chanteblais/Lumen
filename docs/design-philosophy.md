# Coherence — early design philosophy (retired)

Replaced by [Visual Language](design/visual-language.md), [Motion & Interaction](design/motion-and-interaction.md) and the [Experience Principles](philosophy/experience-principles.md). The [original is preserved with relocated links](archive/2026-09-13-before-reconciliation/docs/design-philosophy.md).

The book-only and no-imagery prohibitions no longer govern. Editorial restraint, readable typography and low cognitive burden survive within inhabited places. “Derived, not maintained” means users need not administer the system; it does not prohibit explicit priorities or optional correction. See [Shared terminology](product/shared-model.md).

[Design System](design-system.md) retains implemented tokens and components. [Art Direction](art-direction.md) retains experiments, production rationale and unresolved choices.
