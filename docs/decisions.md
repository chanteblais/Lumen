Append-only. One entry per decision that changes `architecture.md`, `domain.md` or the plan. Format: date · decision · why · what it replaces.

**Product decisions** (what the product is and why, and the constraints they put on future work) live in [`living/decisions.md`](living/decisions.md), the canon's log. An entry here that changes how the product behaves gets a short entry there too.

- **2026-09-13 · Lumi's memory: their word checked in code, beliefs selected per turn, forgetting deletes (`feat/lumi-memory`).** Chanté's brief: continuity across visits — preferences, projects, decisions — with source and time, relevant retrieval instead of the whole store, supersession of stale or contradicted beliefs, a way to inspect, correct and delete, isolation per user, no secrets or sensitive details by default, notes never read as instructions, and chat that survives memory failing. Built on `memory_notes`; no new table or service. (1) **Their word is checked:** `user_said` needs `their_words` found in the user's recent messages, or the belief is held as a guess; guesses start at ≤ 0.6 (v1-gap-plan 2a). (2) **Screens before the insert** (`memory-rules.ts`): secrets and instruction-like text never; a sensitive detail only on their word with an explicit ask; the same thing said again confirms the existing belief; their word for something Lumi guessed supersedes the guess. (3) **Selection per turn** (`memory-select.ts`) replaces "inject all active beliefs, ≤ 40": standing preferences (5) and strategies (3), then lexical relevance to the message, the last few turns and a running session, then the freshest projects and facts, ≤ 12; a guess below 0.5 unconfirmed for 60 days fades from the block (derived at read time); `recall_memory` searches the rest. Embeddings still wait until words stop being enough. (4) **Corrections:** `correct_belief` is the user's revision on their quoted words; Settings' Correct is the same op (v1-gap-plan 2). (5) **Forgetting deletes** — replaces the soft retire with reason `user`: the belief and its supersession chain are removed, `note` is stripped from their `memory.*` events (the one sanctioned rewrite of events: nothing a deleted belief said outlives it), and a `memory.deleted` tombstone keeps one-way content keys so an inference can't bring it back. The transcript is not touched: what the user said stays in the 30-message window until it scrolls out (open question 25). (6) **Framing:** notes are quoted, one line each, under a line saying they are data, not instructions, and never override the rules or the current request. (7) **Failure:** `loadBeliefsOrNothing` — a turn or page carries on without beliefs, and the block tells Lumi not to claim either way. Migration `0003` (`memory_notes.source_message_id`). DB tests run the real migrations on PGlite (new dev dependency, in-process).
