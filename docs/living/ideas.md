## Rewards

**Credit for what ADHD brains rarely get credit for.** Promising. *(Chanté's notes, 2026-09-12)*
- **Showing up:** opening Coherence and meaningfully engaging.
- **Beginning:** starting something, especially something repeatedly deferred.
- **Doing:** completing tasks, focus sessions, milestones.
- **Clarifying:** breaking something down, deciding a next action, organising ambiguity.
- **Choosing:** consciously postponing, delegating or deleting something instead of carrying it indefinitely.
- **Caring:** tending Lumi's environment, checking in with yourself, intentionally resting.
- **Returning:** coming back after an absence.

*Showing up* and *doing* are **Accepted** (decisions → *Rewards only ever give*). The rest are candidates. *Caring* meets the proposed rule that the user never has to tend anything (`art-direction.md` §3); it works only if tending is optional and only ever adds.

**A welcome-back bonus.** Promising. Returning after an absence earns a little extra instead of costing anything. It fits re-entry as a first-class problem.

**Recognise consistency without a streak.** Promising. How to acknowledge a rhythm without turning absence into loss is unanswered.

**Lumi's World.** Exploring. In the Coherence mockup it is a nav item and a card, "a cozy place to grow". See open question 7 and `art-direction.md` §9.

## Lumi

**Work out what's in the way.** Promising. *(Chanté's notes)* "Coherence can help you determine whether the obstacle is ambiguity, emotional resistance, scope, dependency, exhaustion, or simply this isn't actually something you want to do." Close to the *Not this* reasons, and to `product.md`'s first pillar (diagnose whether the task is unclear or starting is the barrier). The last case leads naturally to *Choosing*: letting it go.

**Something alongside you that remembers where you've been.** Exploring. *(Chanté's notes)* "Something alongside you that remembers where you've been, notices what keeps emerging, and helps translate those things into your actual life when appropriate." Possibly the heart of the Library (open question 3).

**Lumi works alongside during focus.** Promising. She reads or writes quietly while a session runs: body doubling made visible. `art-direction.md` bet 6.

**Lumi as a resident of Home.** Exploring. She stands and moves in the painted room, not only in the corner. `art-direction.md` §6, bet 4.

**A layered "puppet" Lumi.** Exploring. One drawing cut into parts that code moves. `art-direction.md` bet 2.
