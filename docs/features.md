## Navigation

The rail and its parchment (`components/shell/Sidebar.tsx`), present on every page once you're signed in (not on sign-in or sign-up, which take the full width: the nav belongs to the space you enter) — a green rail with an icon per place, and the names on a sheet of parchment that opens from it (2026-09-13, Chanté's ask, after `art/mockups/rail-nav.png`):

```
Coherence   Home · Today · Library  ·  Lists · Insights  ·  Settings
```

- The place you're in: its icon lit on the rail and a brass star on the rail's rule beside it (`aria-current="page"`).
- **Hover** the rail (or tab into it) and the parchment slides out over the page — the wordmark, the names beside their icons, *Progress lives here.* — and folds when the pointer leaves. **Click the top half of the rail** (the star's half) or the parchment and it stays **pinned** out, floating over the page as on hover (the page never moves for it); click again to fold it. Any spot there that isn't a link pins — the star, the rail between the icons, beside a name, the head, the foot. **Click the bottom half of the rail** (the moon's half) and the names stay locked in, even on hover, until that half is clicked again or the nav is pinned (keyboard focus still shows them). Pinned, locked or neither is remembered in this browser (a cookie), so the nav is right from the first paint. Nothing depends on either.
- Mobile (<768px): the nav is a green bar along the bottom of the screen, the rail on its side — the six icons, each with its name under it, the place you're in lit with a brass star over it. Nothing opens, pins or locks on a phone; the page stops above the bar.
- Nav carries **no counts, no badges, no dots** — by design (`design-philosophy.md` §3.2).
- **Every page is current when you see it** (2026-09-13). Pages are read from the database each time they open. Going Back or Forward shows the page at once and then swaps in anything that changed since (you ticked something off with Lumi on Home, went back to Today: it's gone); so does coming back to a tab left open a minute or more (`FreshOnReturn.tsx`). What you're typing and where you've scrolled stay put. Nothing for the user to do or keep.
- The browser tab is a running head too: *Today · Coherence*, *Lists · Coherence*, *Library · Coherence*, *Insights · Coherence*, *Settings · Coherence*; Home is just *Coherence* (`layout.tsx` title template, each page's `metadata.title`).
- Decided 2026-09-12: V1 nav is **Chat · Today · Lists · Settings**; Chat stays the homepage (`today.md` → Decisions). Library and Insights leave the nav when Lists lands (M3). **Insights returns the same day** with a real job — what Lumi noticed in the mail — so the nav is **Chat · Today · Lists · Insights · Settings**. **Renamed 2026-09-12:** the first item is **Home** — it is where you land and where Lumi is; *Chat* named the mechanism, not the place. Same route (`/`), same page; the nav reads **Home · Today · Lists · Insights · Settings**. **Renamed 2026-09-13 (Chanté's ask, with its painting):** *Lists* is the **Library** — route `/library`, and `/lists` redirects there permanently (`next.config.ts`); the nav reads **Home · Today · Library · Insights · Settings**. The lists inside it keep their names. **Lists comes back the same day (Chanté's ask, with `art/mockups/lists-mockup.png`)**, as a sixth icon that opens a sheet over the page you're on rather than a room (Lists, below); `/lists` no longer redirects. It first sat after Today; on review Chanté moved it below the Library and asked for a break there, to **separate the rooms from the tools**: the nav reads **Home · Today · Library** | **Lists · Insights** | **Settings**, each break a gap with a soft brass hairline on the rail. **While mail is switched off (2026-09-13, Insights below)** Insights isn't in the nav: **Home · Today · Library** | **Lists** | **Settings**, five icons on the phone's bar.

**Lumi in the corner** (`components/shell/LumiCompanion.tsx`): full figure standing on the bottom-right edge of every page (on Today and in the Library, on a wide window, standing in the painting instead) — breathing and blinking, holding nothing — small movements. Since 2026-09-13 she is hands-free most of the time: the lantern is something she can pick up, not something she carries. Now and then her hands come together in front of her and part again. Her shadow is drawn to suit where she stands: a soft shadow under her feet everywhere, and in the Today and Library paintings a longer one cast away from the room's light, with the room's warm light on her. **She waves when you arrive** (2026-09-13): open the app, or come back to its tab, after half an hour or more away — or for the first time in a browser — and a moment later she raises a hand, waves twice and goes back to breathing. Once per arrival; moving between pages doesn't count. Still under reduced-motion (changing the setting takes effect at once, no reload). She doesn't animate for no one: in a hidden tab or under the Lists sheet she waits, and carries on when she's seen. **Tap her and a speech bubble opens** (`CompanionBubble.tsx`): one text box (*Tell Lumi…*), Enter sends, so you can say "add take out compost" from Today or the Library without leaving the page. The message goes through `/api/chat` like any other, so it lands in the main conversation; what you said slides in as a bubble of its own, Lumi blinks, and the box reads *Lumi's on it…* until her reply and the ledger of what she did show beneath (if she stays silent, nothing shows beneath what you said and the box goes back to *Anything else?*); the page underneath refreshes once the turn has landed. While she talks the send arrow is a **stop** (a small square): tap it and she stops mid-sentence — no further writing, no further tools — and what she had said stays (in the bubble and in the conversation). Home's composer does the same. The bubble never runs off the window: it opens above her when there's room, and beside her (level with her hood, growing down) where she stands high in a painting, as on Today; a long reply scrolls inside it, following her words unless you've scrolled up to reread. Escape (focus goes back to her), a click elsewhere or a tap on her closes it — **without stopping her**: a turn in flight finishes and the page still refreshes when it lands, and opening the bubble again while she's still going shows the exchange. Once it has landed the bubble opens fresh; nothing is kept. On Home, tapping her just focuses the composer. Hidden with her under 768px. In development only, a strip of small buttons above her (her hands, the wave, picking up a book and setting it down, and a blink) cues each on demand so it can be checked without waiting for the schedule, and folds away behind a small handle when not wanted; it never ships.

## Auth

Protected-first (`src/proxy.ts`): every route requires sign-in except `/sign-in` and `/sign-up`. `requireUser()` (`src/lib/auth.ts`) resolves the Clerk session to the internal `users` row — created lazily on first visit with the Clerk first name and the browser timezone (cookie `coherence_tz`, set by `TimezoneCapture` in the layout; a first visit refreshes once so "today" is right). A page open or a chat turn calls `requireVisit()` instead, which finds the row and bumps `last_seen_at` in one query and returns the previous value for the greeting. No onboarding screen: signing in *is* onboarding.

## Pages

### Home (`/`) — the soft landing (the nav item was *Chat* until 2026-09-12; "chat" below means the conversation)

**Who:** Signed-in users.
**What:** The conversation with Lumi. The only surface that matters in V1.

- **The room** (2026-09-12, Chanté's ask) — Home is set in a painted room (`art/scenery/home/background.png`, served as `public/home-room.webp`): the painting fills the viewport behind the shell (`.home-scene`), dimmed toward the spine and the floor so the type sits in the light, and the whole page takes that light — parchment type on dark glass, the composer the same pill on dark glass with the send button as the lamp, Lumi's lines on dark plates and yours on a tint. The greeting hangs on the wall as a parchment scroll (ink on paper, wooden rods, rounded corners) with its quick starts inside it; since 2026-09-13 (Chanté's ask) it is ~18% shorter and its parchment warmer and a little translucent, so the room still reads through it. Tap its bottom rod and the scroll rolls up to its title line between the rods, leaving more of the room and the conversation in view; tap again to unroll. It stays rolled only for this page open — the next visit opens unrolled, and nothing is kept. Only Home is lamplit: Today and the Library have paintings of their own (below); Insights and Settings stay ivory. One layer and a re-mapped palette (`design-system.md` → Home: the room); nothing for the user to keep.
- **Greeting** (`components/chat/Greeting.tsx`, a chapter opening on bare paper — on Home, on the scroll — headpiece, portrait, title line, second line, chips; no box — see `design-system.md`) — two lines from `core/ai/greeting.ts` (deterministic, no model call): recognition ("Good to see you, Chanté.") + somewhere to begin. The blank question ("What are we working with today?") is asked **once** — only while there is no conversation to pick up. After that the second line continues from the last thing said, by *when* it was in the user's own calendar, never by what is undone: same day → "Welcome back, Chanté." / "Where did we end up?" (within five minutes: "Still here."); yesterday → "Picking up from yesterday. What's first today?"; two to six days → "It's been a few days. Where did we leave things?"; a week or more (by visit *or* by conversation) → "It's been a minute. Want me to help figure out what's still relevant?"; an abandoned session ("Looks like we left a session open on *X*. Pick it back up, or let it go?", M5) comes first and stays until you've said anything since it was closed. The visit gap is the one this *sitting* began after (M4: derived from the newest `app.opened`), so opening Today first and then Chat still gets the re-entry line; it stays until you've said something this sitting.
- **Coming back** (M4) — on a re-entry sitting the context block tells Lumi the greeting offered the pass, on every turn of the visit; saying "yes" (or asking) runs it: the stale intentions by name in one short pass, drops in one go, one suggested next step, never a count. Letting things go re-cuts Today's path (`reason: reentry`).
- **Not this, answered** (M4) — the six quick answers from Today arrive as one visible message ("Not this one: *title* — too big.") with the reason in metadata; the route records `intention.declined`, the context block carries the reason under *Just now*, and Lumi answers the reason (persona → Not this: smallest piece · easiest win · one clarifying question · no persuasion · what instead · "Fair."). The path is re-cut around it after the reply, without narration.
- **Lumi knows what you just did on the pages** (2026-09-12, Chanté's ask: she ticked something off by mistake, asked Lumi to add it back, and Lumi asked what it was) — every turn's context block carries *Recent changes*: the last 36 hours of intention changes wherever they happened, each with who did it (*they ticked "X" done on Today or Lists* vs *you marked "X" done*), the id, and where the thing stands now; *Recently done* carries ids too. The persona says: "the one I just checked off" is in there — act on it, don't ask. The new `reopen_intention` tool puts a done or dropped thing back (ledger line *Back on the list · title*) instead of creating a duplicate. Nothing to maintain: the events already existed.
- **Lumi knows the app, and where you are** (2026-09-13, Chanté's ask: "aware of her environment and the app's functionality") — every turn tells her the page you spoke from and which way in: Home's conversation, the bubble on another page, or the Lists sheet's Add task (`core/places.ts`; the client sends the path and its chat slot, the route trusts only a known place). The context block says it in one line under the time (*Where they are: Today, with today's path in front of them, talking to you through the bubble…*), so "this one feels too big" on Today is the Right now, and a line from Add task gets filed with a few words back. Her persona carries a short map of the places, what can be done by hand in each, the rooms she lives in, and what isn't in the app yet (reminders, attachments, name/timezone/session-length settings, session history, making or renaming lists, browsing the Library), so "how do I…" gets the one place and the one tap, or a plain "not yet" — never an invented button. She doesn't remark on where you are, and knows the page, not the screen. Nothing to maintain: nothing is stored, and a turn without a place (an old tab, a malformed body) simply has no line.
- **Focus Together** (M5, `components/focus/SessionBar.tsx`, `core/focus.ts`, `core/domain/sessions.ts`) — company while you work. You never set a session up: "Body double", *Start with Lumi* from Today, or "stay with me" in your own words, and Lumi settles what · first step · how long (taking what the context already knows — the intention's next step and estimate — rather than re-asking), calls `start_focus_session`, and says one line ("Open the doc, read the last paragraph. I'm here."). From Today it is immediate: the route tells her the tap was the start itself, with the path's first step and the estimate, so there is nothing to ask; a second tap while that session runs gets one line, not a second session. Ledger: *Together · goal · 45 min*. A **bar** then sits above the composer while it runs: *Together* · the goal · *First: the step* · *12 of 45 min* (minutes in, never a countdown) · **End**. Lumi is quiet unless you speak. Every check-in interval (`preferences.check_in_minutes`, 15 by default), and at the planned end when that comes sooner, the interface — not Lumi — asks **Still with it?** in a small card above the bar (at or past the planned length: *That's the time we set. Keep going?*) with four chips: **Yep** (an event, nothing else — the card just goes), **Stuck**, **Got distracted**, **Done**. The last three land in the transcript as what you tapped ("Stuck." / "Got distracted." / "Done.") and Lumi answers in a line or two: the smallest next action · "Welcome back. Where did we end up?" · one line and no stats. Saying anything else while the card is up dismisses it. **Done** and **End** close the session in code before Lumi sees the message — no "did you finish?" — Done as completed, End as stopped early (visible text "Let's stop here."); if the thing itself is finished she completes the intention, asking only when it changes what she'd do next. Saying "I'm done" in your own words works too (`end_focus_session`). No stats, no streaks, no session history page. **Left open:** a session past twice its planned length is closed as abandoned on your next visit (any page) and Chat greets you with "Looks like we left a session open on *X*. Pick it back up, or let it go?" until you've said anything; "pick it back up" starts it again with the same goal. Chat only — Today and Lists show nothing about a running session (the companion is enough; an idle timer is a small pressure).
- **Lumi learns from the session, quietly** (M5, `core/ai/reflect.ts`) — when a session ends, after the reply has streamed, reflection runs once: a completed session whose approach matches a strategy belief confirms it ("helped 2/2"), an abandoned one contradicts it, and one model call may add a little more under code guardrails (see `architecture.md` → Reflection). Nothing to rate; visible later in "What Lumi knows" (M6).
- **Lumi remembers the visit, and files what mattered** (2026-09-13, `core/ai/consolidate.ts`) — once a visit is over (or partway through a long one), after a reply or when Home opens, off the response: one model call writes a short memory of the visit and files what was meaningful under Library threads, rewriting their summaries. Later turns carry the recent visits that have scrolled out of view and the threads a message touches (Supporting Features → The Library). Nothing shows while it happens; nothing to rate or review.
- **Asking reshapes Today** (2026-09-12, Chanté's ask: "I need an easy task" got an easy task in chat, but Today stayed as it was) — when the user asks for a different shape of day ("something easy", "quick wins", "what should I do now", a replan), Lumi picks the thing in her reply and calls `reshape_today` with the ask and her pick; after the reply, Today's path is re-cut around the ask (`reason: asked`) with her pick pinned as Right now, so the page shows what she just said (ledger line *Reshaped Today · something easy*). The pin wins over a decline earlier today. Not narrated; nothing new to maintain — the ask is words the user already typed.
- **Quick starts** — Help me choose · Break it down · Body double · Just talk, plus "another way in" (sends "I don't know where to start."). Each chip sends its own label as the user message — the user sees exactly what they tapped — and the persona knows what each means. Shown until you've said something *this sitting* (no message in the last thirty minutes — `isInSitting`, the same gap that starts a visit in the events), then the greeting compacts to a running head; they return next time you come back.
- **Ledger lines** (`components/chat/Ledger.tsx`, the lines from `core/ai/ledger.ts`) — under a reply, a quiet ✦ line per thing Lumi did: *Noted · title · list*, *Updated · title*, *Done · title*, *Back on the list · title*, *Let go · title*, *Remembered · …* (*Noticed · …* when it's her reading; nothing when she already knew it), *Corrected · …*, *Forgotten*, *Kept for title · …* (the Library), *Today · not much*, *Together · goal · 45 min*, *Session closed · goal*. Rendered from tool parts. One line per thing that happened to the user, not per tool call (2026-09-13, Chanté: two things she added showed *Noted* and then *Updated* each): an update to a thing noted in the same reply folds into its *Noted* line, and an update that changed nothing (the tool wrote nothing) shows nothing.
- **Every open feels fresh** (M4, Chanté's ask) — the transcript is in two parts: everything that was there when the page opened, then the greeting (a chapter head: headpiece ornament, portrait, title line), then what you say now. The page opens scrolled to the greeting, so Chat reads as new each time you come to it, and the earlier conversation is one scroll up, untouched. It is there from the first paint (2026-09-13 fix: the scroll used to wait for React, so on Home the old chat showed for a second and then slid away; an inline script in the layout, `OPEN_ON_CARD_SCRIPT`, now places it as the HTML is read). The page arrives whole (no Suspense), so the first paint is already the room and its lamplit palette, never a flash of the ivory shell; the painting comes into focus over a blurred copy of itself. From your first message on, it follows the newest line like any chat. **Scroll past the end** (2026-09-13, Chanté's ask) and the whole conversation leaves the top of the view, so on Home only the room and the composer are left; it is all one scroll up, and the next line brings it back. Nothing is hidden or kept — there's just room to scroll. The greeting is full (with quick starts) after thirty minutes of quiet, compact within a sitting. The date rules between messages keep their six-hour window.
- **Conversation** (`components/chat/Conversation.tsx`, `useChat`) — the transcript around the greeting. User lines right-aligned on paper-deep; Lumi lines with a 36px avatar. A line said this visit settles onto the page (a short rise and fade, `.chat-now`); what was there when the page opened is simply there. A fine rule with "Yesterday" / "3 days ago" separates messages more than six hours apart. A reply's text blocks (a line, then a tool call, then another line) render as separate paragraphs, not run together. While Lumi is thinking, three slow dots. When she says nothing (a *yep* during a focus session needs no answer), nothing shows: no empty line, the dots go, the composer is free again, and nothing is saved to the transcript (2026-09-13). Errors render as one line in Lumi's voice ("I lost the thread for a second. Say that again?"). The last 30 messages load on page open; the client sends only the new message and the server holds the transcript. **Leaving Home while Lumi is mid-turn doesn't stop her** (2026-09-13): her tools finish and the page you went to refreshes when she lands; come back while she's still going and the turn is there, arriving below the greeting; come back after and Home opens on the transcript as the server has it.
- **Composer** — pinned to the bottom of the viewport; the transcript above scrolls in its own region (`.chat-page` / `.chat-scroll` / `.composer-dock`), so the input is always in reach and the layout is identical on first load, after a turn, and after navigating away and back. Auto-growing textarea and send (the `+` that sat before the textarea did nothing and was removed, 2026-09-13). Enter sends, Shift+Enter newlines; disabled while a turn streams. On a wide page (2026-09-13, Chanté's ask) the pill is ~70% of the column's width, centred under the greeting rather than stretching edge to edge; on a phone it keeps the full width.
- **Voice** (`useVoiceInput`, `components/chat/voice/`) — the mic link under the composer toggles listening; the composer border turns brass and the placeholder says "Listening…". Transcribed text lands in the textarea (appended to anything already typed) for the user to read and send — nothing is sent automatically. Two engines behind the one button, chosen automatically: the browser's **Web Speech API** (Chrome, Safari, Edge), or **Whisper running locally** in a worker (Brave, Firefox — Brave exposes the API but ships no speech service behind it, so every session failed with `network`). The local engine asks for the mic, then loads the model while already capturing, so nothing said during the wait is lost; states are *Getting voice ready…* (first tap downloads ~90 MB once; afterwards under two seconds) → *Listening…* (text refreshes every ~4 s) → *Writing that down…* (one final pass, under a second). With the local engine, audio never leaves the device; the Web Speech engine sends it to the browser's maker for recognition (Google for Chrome, Apple for Safari), and the privacy page says so (corrected 2026-09-13). A silent take says "I didn't hear anything from ‹mic name›. Is that the right mic?" rather than inventing words — the name is there because macOS may have picked a nearby iPhone. The Web Speech engine treats the tap as intent and outlives the browser's own session ends: a plain end restarts quietly and keeps the transcript; a session that ends instantly twice gives up with one line. Microphone blocked, no network, ~8s of silence (`no-speech`), or another tab/app taking the mic (`aborted`) → one line in Lumi's voice under the composer, never a silent switch-off. Every recognition error is `console.warn`ed as `[voice]`; `localStorage.coherence.voice = "local" | "speech"` forces an engine for testing. The microphone is the browser's default input device (Chrome: `chrome://settings/content/microphone`; macOS may offer a nearby iPhone as a Continuity mic) — the page cannot choose one, and we don't add a picker.
- **Tool links** — Add file · Voice · Tools. M0: visual. Voice lands in M7; Add file and Tools are placeholders (may be cut).
- **Status:** M0 built 2026-09-11 (static). M1: greeting uses the signed-in first name and the real visit gap. M2 (2026-09-12): live conversation with `claude-opus-5` (Lumi's model since 2026-09-13: `gpt-6-astra`, `architecture.md` → Model), persisted; no tools yet — Lumi says so if asked to remember or track something. Avatar is Lumi's head (neutral) from `mockups/lumi.png` as of 2026-09-11.

### Today (`/today`) — spec: `today.md` — **built M3 (2026-09-12); capacity + Not this M4 (2026-09-12)**

**Who:** Signed-in.
**What:** Answers *what should I be doing right now?* The running head (*Today* kicker + divider), the greeting by time of day, Lumi's one-line read of the day, one dominant **Right now** (title, list · estimate as a marginal note in small caps, the first physical step, **Start with Lumi**, then *Not this* · *Break it down* · *Done*), up to three **After that** rows, **Later** (fixed-time commitments only), and "everything else can wait." The plan is generated once per day — primed in the background when the app is opened or a chat turn ends, so the page usually finds it ready and arrives whole, the card with it (2026-09-13: it used to stream in behind a placeholder even when ready, and the card showed up a beat after the page); otherwise generated on first open, streamed in under Suspense (the card with Lumi's three slow dots, the same pause as in the chat) — persisted, and stable across reloads (an empty plan is re-cut once intentions exist); *Done* on the card, or the circle on an After-that row, completes it and advances the path in code: the first After-that row becomes Right now with a plain first step (*Pick it up where it opens.*), the day line stays, and nothing is said. Nothing past the After-that rows is shown — no collapsed line, no number; the closing line is the only sign there's more. Asking in Chat for a different shape ("something easy") re-cuts the path around the ask after Lumi's reply, with the thing she named as Right now. **Start with Lumi / Break it down** are links into Chat that send a visible message ("Let's start: *title*") with the intention id in metadata.

- **The garden** (2026-09-12, Chanté's ask; laid out after `art/mockups/today-mockup.png`) — Today is set in a painted greenhouse (`art/scenery/today/background.png`, served as `public/today-room.webp`). The page sits on the right in three layers, each quieter than the last (2026-09-13, Chanté's ask — the one tall panel read as a list pasted on a painting): Lumi's words set straight on the painting (the greeting, her day line and, once a day, the capacity question), the one Right now card, and a faint slip with After that, Later and *Everything else can wait* as its last line. The room is left open to their left. On a wide window (from 1100px) Lumi leaves the corner and stands in the greenhouse, on the flagstones behind the raised bed; tap her there for the bubble as anywhere. The clock sits on the painting. Same content, same order; nothing taken from the mockup that would add a thing to manage (`design-system.md` → Today: the garden); nothing for the user to keep.
- **Capacity prompt** (`components/today/CapacityPrompt.tsx`, M4) — one quiet line above the card, under Lumi's day line on the painting, *How much have we got today?* — Not much · Normal-ish · Lots · Skip, as words rather than buttons. Shown only when today has no capacity report (from here or from chat), hasn't been skipped today, and there is a path to shape. Answering posts to `/api/capacity`, shows "Shaping the day around that…" while the path is re-cut (a few seconds), and the page refreshes with the new Right now; *Normal-ish* on a plan that already assumed normal changes nothing. Skip records `capacity.asked` and the line goes away for the day. Never blocking, never a metric.
- **Not this** (`components/today/RightNowActions.tsx`, M4) — tapping it swaps the button row for Lumi's fixed question, *Fair. What's getting in the way?*, and six chips: Too big · Too tired · Don't know how · Don't feel like it · Something else is more important · Just nope, plus a quiet *Keep it* to close. A chip hands off into Chat (`/?decline=<id>&reason=<key>`) as one visible message with the reason; Lumi replies to it and the path is re-cut around the answer (the declined thing is never Right now again today). Resistance adapts the plan; nothing is recorded as failure.
- A page open counts as a visit (`requireVisit`), so a sitting can begin on Today.

### Lists (`/lists`) — a sheet from the nav — **built 2026-09-13** (`feat/lists-sheet`)

**Who:** Signed-in.
**What:** Everything on your lists, on one sheet of parchment, after `art/mockups/lists-mockup.png` (`components/lists/ListsSheet.tsx`). The rail's list icon (the first of the tools, below the Library) opens it **over the page you're on**, which stays underneath: the sheet is a parallel slot intercepting `/lists` (`app/@sheet/(.)lists`), so the URL is `/lists` and Back, ×, Escape or a click beside the sheet return to that page. Opened directly (a link, a reload), `/lists` shows the sheet over the Library's room, and × goes to the Library. The rail stays beside it and the parchment with the names still slides out over it. Lumi steps out of view while it's open (she would stand on its rows); the sheet's Add task speaks to her. From the keyboard it behaves as a dialog: focus moves into it, Tab stays inside it, and closing puts focus back where it was (the rail's list icon). The rail stays clickable beside it.

- **Head** — a sprig, *Lists*, *All the moving pieces, in one place.*, a search box and **Add task**.
- **Choosing what it shows** — tabs across the top (All · each of your lists · Completed) and, beside the rows, a side column with the same plus **Quick views**: *Today* (has today's date) and *Due soon* (dated within the next seven days). The two always agree. It opens on All each time; nothing is remembered. **No numbers anywhere** — no per-list counts, no Completed tally — and **no Overdue**, no *No due date*, no sort or filter (Chanté's call, 2026-09-13: the mockup had all of these). On a phone the side column goes and the quick views join the tabs.

## Supporting Features

### Intentions (M3)
"Intention", not task: something the user meant to do. Created, updated, completed, reopened and dropped by Lumi through tools (`docs/architecture.md` → Tools) or ticked/unticked on Today/Library; either way Lumi sees it in her next turn's *Recent changes*, with who did it. Fields: title, next action, note, list, estimate, due. Status open/done/dropped; stale (14 days untouched) is derived. Every change is an event.

### Beliefs — the understanding layer (M3 tools; session reflection M5; daily reflection M6)
`memory_notes` with kind · confidence · evidence · where it came from. Lumi calls `remember` for durable things the user says (`user_said`, with their words quoted and checked — otherwise it's held as her guess) or notices (`lumi_inferred`, ≤ 0.6), `confirm/contradict` as evidence arrives, `revise_belief` for her own guesses, and `correct_belief` / `forget_belief` when the user corrects something or asks her to forget it (on their quoted words; forgetting deletes). `core/domain/memory.ts` applies the ops with guardrails (caps, user-said beliefs untouchable by Lumi, retire below 0.2) and the rules in `memory-rules.ts`: never passwords, codes, keys or ID numbers, never a note that reads as an instruction, no duplicates. Each turn carries **up to 12 beliefs chosen for it** (`core/ai/memory-select.ts`: how they like her to be, what helps them start, what the conversation is about, the freshest projects), quoted and marked whose word they are; `recall_memory` searches the rest. If beliefs can't be read, the conversation carries on without them. Since M5, ending a focus session also runs reflection (`core/ai/reflect.ts`): the session's approach confirms or contradicts the matching strategy belief in code, and one structured-output model call may add a little more under guardrails (`source: reflection`, ≤ 0.6). Inspect, correct and forget: Settings → *What Lumi knows* (2026-09-13).

### The Library — memory that consolidates (2026-09-13, `feat/lumi-library`)
Recent conversation lives on after it scrolls away, and what mattered is filed where it belongs. After each visit (or partway through a long one), Lumi quietly writes a short memory of it — what you talked about, where it was left — and files what was meaningful under **threads**: the subjects that run through your life, like a book you're writing. Each thread has a summary she keeps rewriting and a growing archive of notes (ideas, decisions, open questions, progress, details), each marked as your words or her reading; a note that changed is kept as history, not shown as current. A thread appears only for a subject you spent real time on or came back to; a passing mention stays in that visit's memory.

Next time a thread comes up — by its name or the words you use for it — Lumi has its summary and the notes that bear on what you just said, a list of the other threads she keeps, and the recent visits that have scrolled out of view. In words: "keep that for the book" (ledger *Kept for The book · …*), "what did I decide about the ending?" (she searches), "forget the plum tree idea" or "forget the Lisbon trip" (deleted and not filed again; the conversation itself stays). Nothing to set up, file, tag or review.

**Sections** (2026-09-13, `feat/library-sections`, Chanté's ask: "Threads that become categories should gain their own section in the library"). A **section** is like a category: yoga, cooking, the book they're writing, an area of focus — something they keep mentioning in various ways (Chanté). Threads fall under it. It is itself a thread, one with threads under it: "Coherence", holding "Memory design" and "Onboarding". Inside a section, a thread that gathers threads of its own is a **shelf** ("Memory & Continuity"), and the threads are its **books**, titled with the thread's name. Three levels at most. Lumi does the shelving between visits, conservatively: only loose threads move, a thread once shelved stays put, and a new section appears only when it gathers two loose threads. In words: "that goes with the book", "put onboarding under Coherence", "it's its own thing" (`shelve_thread`, on their word; Lumi never undoes where they put something). Lumi knows where each thread sits, so "where did we put that thing about domains?" has an answer. The Library page shows it (Library, above).

### Focus sessions (M5)
`focus_sessions`: goal, first step, approach, planned and check-in minutes, outcome `completed | stopped_early | abandoned`. Started only by Lumi (`start_focus_session`), one at a time; ended by a Done/End tap (code), by Lumi when asked in words (`end_focus_session`), or by the abandonment sweep on the next visit. Every start, check-in and end is an event; `approach` on the session and `intention_id` on the events are what let reflection link a way in to its outcome. Defaults from `users.preferences` (45 min, check-ins every 15); no settings UI yet (M6).

### Capacity (M3 tool; Today prompt M4)
`report_capacity` when the user says how much they've got, or the prompt on Today (once a day, skippable). Today's capacity is the latest report in the local day; it shapes the plan (≤1 after-that on a low day) and either source re-cuts the path once — from chat, after the reply, in the route's `after()`. The persona is told not to ask when a capacity is already in the context.

### Leads — what Lumi noticed (2026-09-12)
A lead is something Lumi noticed that might need doing (in the mail, for now) that the user hasn't confirmed — see `docs/domain.md` → `leads`. Model proposes, `clampLeads` guards (`core/ai/leads.ts`), the user answers once on Insights or in chat. Kept → an intention with the lead's title, why, list and due; let go → resolved, no reason asked. A message never produces a lead twice; a title already on the list, or kept/let go in the last month, is never suggested again. `docs/architecture.md` → §9.

### Re-entry (M4)
A sitting that begins after ≥ 7 days away (derived from `app.opened`, `core/domain/users.ts`) is a re-entry: greeting offer on Chat, the coming-back pass in conversation (persona → Coming back), stale intentions flagged in the context block and in the planner's inputs, the planner told to keep the path short and fresh, and a re-cut when the pass lets things go. In conversation Lumi may say how long they've been away when it helps them get their bearings, never so it sounds owed (2026-09-13). Nothing on any page shows how long it's been or how much is undone.
